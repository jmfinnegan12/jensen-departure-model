Will Jensen Huang leave as Nvidia CEO?

Rules

Market: KXCEONVDA on Kalshi · contract terms

  • Yes if Jensen Huang announces, or it is announced on his behalf, that he has left his position as Nvidia's chief executive, before the date.
  • He has to have actually left. Announcing a future departure does not count.
  • The reason is not a condition. The terms do not distinguish resigning, being removed, or retiring.
  • Huang only. The market covers the chief executive at the time it was listed.
  • "Before" excludes the date itself.
  • Each date is judged separately, so more than one can resolve Yes. A market closes early once he has left.
  • A leave of absence or an interim chief executive isn't addressed. The exchange decides borderline cases.
  • Sources named in the terms: Huang, Nvidia and Bloomberg.
  • Open dates: Jan 1, 2027; Jul 1, 2027; Jan 1, 2028; Jan 1, 2029; Jan 1, 2031; Jan 1, 2033.
