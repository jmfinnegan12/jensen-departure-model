Will Jensen Huang leave as Nvidia CEO?

Question Model

GitHub · suggest a change · how to contribute

I chose this as the second event because it sits at the opposite end of the spectrum from the first on a lot of dimensions. It is topical, in an active news cycle, with a lot of uncertainty. It is anchored in real, fast moving events, as opposed to potentially unanswerable, deep human questions.

What happened

  • Oct 29, 2026. The New York Times reports that U.S. spy agencies had watched Alice Huang, an executive of Megaspeed,2 a Singapore-based company that bought Nvidia's advanced chips.1 Jensen Huang tells Bloomberg his dealings with her were business-related and "not unusual". The two are not related.1
  • Nov 5. Three House committee chairs ask him for a closed-door briefing on Nov 18.4
  • Nov 6 to 8. The Financial Times reports that the U.S. government believes the two had a romantic relationship.1 The Times reports a memo describing her as an asset of China's Ministry of State Security, recruited to gather information on him.2
  • Nov 8. He tells staff by email: "I was unfaithful to my wife."3 Nvidia's board opens an independent investigation and says it "has no reason to believe that either national security or NVIDIA secrets were compromised".5
  • Nov 9. President Trump says Huang told him he had no idea she was a spy,2 and that he should stay.1 China's Foreign Ministry calls the spy allegation "unsubstantiated".1
  • Nov 10. The board's independent directors hire Joon H. Kim, a former acting U.S. attorney, to run the investigation.7 The chair of the House Foreign Affairs Committee says he would subpoena Huang if he does not answer by Nov 11.8
  • Nov 11. The New York Times reports a second relationship, with Kimberly Powell, an Nvidia vice president who reports directly to him; Nvidia declines to comment.9 The Wall Street Journal reports that the board is preparing to oust him.10

The question

Will Huang have left his position as chief executive, and will that have been announced, before a set date? He has to have actually left: announcing a future departure does not count. Sixteen dates are open, from Nov 12, 2026 to Jan 1, 2033.6 Rules →
